### 🚀 Featured: V3RON — POS & business management for bars

A Flutter + Supabase point-of-sale system **running live in production** at a bar in Pune, handling real daily billing, inventory, staff roles and customer credit.

- **Multi-tenant Postgres schema** on Supabase, built to serve bars, restaurants and liquor shops from one codebase via feature flags
- **Append-only credit (udhaar) ledger** with FIFO repayment and advance balances, rebuilt after tracing a production desync bug
- **Excise-aware item model** that separates the menu hierarchy from Maharashtra excise classes
- **After-midnight business days:** UTC storage, IST display, configurable day-start offset
- **Subscription gating** for paid modules through Razorpay

`Flutter` `Dart` `Supabase` `PostgreSQL` `Razorpay`  ·  [Live site →](https://aditya-bhoye.github.io/Veron/)
